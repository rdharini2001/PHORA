PHORA treats the LR-1–LR-5 continuum as ordered severity, with LR-M and LR-TIV handled through dedicated pathways. Named imaging concepts shape the decision at each stage.

01 — THE IMAGE REPRESENTATION

From voxels to physiological evidence

Four CT phases and a lesion annotation are distilled into morphology, radiomic texture, local context, and multiphase enhancement trajectories.

~900 candidate imaging features → 140 screened variables
PHORA uses a 175-dimensional interpretable tabular representation (140 selected imaging variables, 11 clinical concept/burden estimates, and 24 interaction terms). The diagram describes the published processing stages; it is a representation of the model’s structured feature flow.
