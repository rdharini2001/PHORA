02 / INSIDE PHORA

Reasoning that follows
clinical structure.

LI-RADS is not a flat sequence of unrelated diagnoses. PHORA distinguishes ordered severity from special-category evidence, exposing recognizable intermediate signals along the way.

01 — THE IMAGE REPRESENTATION

From voxels to physiological evidence

Four CT phases and a lesion annotation are distilled into morphology, radiomic texture, local context, and multiphase enhancement trajectories.

~900 candidate imaging features → 140 screened variables
PHORA uses a 175-dimensional interpretable tabular representation (140 selected imaging variables, 11 clinical concept/burden estimates, and 24 interaction terms). The diagram describes the published processing stages; it is not a neural attention visualization.
